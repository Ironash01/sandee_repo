# Attribution and license checklist

Only the **Little Robot Sound Factory 8-Bit Sound Effects Library** requires attribution.

Suggested in-game credits / README text:

> "8-Bit Sound Effects Library" by Little Robot Sound Factory, licensed under CC BY 3.0.
> Source: https://opengameart.org/content/8-bit-sound-effects-library
> Changes: trimmed, pitch-shifted, equalized, and mixed for SandEE.

Also link **www.littlerobotsoundfactory.com** where possible.
