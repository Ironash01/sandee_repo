**Scope:** This reference lists the interactive elements available in SandEE. Figure numbers refer to `SandEE_User_Manual_Draft.docx`; screenshots are in `manual_assets/screenshots/`.

## 9. Missing or Inaccessible Interactions

- No Google/OAuth sign-in control.
- No email registration field.
- No password-recovery or reset control.
- No cloud-save upload button; uploads are automatic.
- No live leaderboard row capture.
- No account-switch control capture.
- No physical Android hardware capture.
- No later Lua dungeon or side-mission capture.
